About Me

Hello, my name is Bryce Benson. I'm a Senior Computer Science student from Richmond, Virginia. As a senior Computer Science student at North Carolina A&T State University with a solid academic foundation and practical experience, I am confident in my ability to make a meaningful contribution to your team. During my time at NC A&T, I have developed expertise in programming languages such as Python, Java, C++, and HTML, in addition to coursework in areas like Database Design, Operating Systems, and AI & Machine Learning. My hands-on experience includes serving as a Cybersecurity Researcher at the Center of Excellence in Cybersecurity Research, Education, and Outreach (CREO), where I conducted research on cyberattack solutions for water infrastructures during natural disasters. Additionally, I have strengthened my problem-solving and technical support skills as a Client Technology Service Assistant, addressing a wide range of technical issues in IT support environments. My internship at the Society for Human Resource Management (SHRM) allowed me to manage complex software and hardware support tasks, including password resets, system configurations, and computer provisioning. This role also improved my ability to communicate effectively while resolving technical issues, supporting SHRM’s extensive employee network and ensuring efficient operations. Alongside my technical skills, I have a strong background in leadership, critical thinking, and collaboration, honed through my involvement with organizations such as the National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE) and the Association for Computer Machinery (ACM).
